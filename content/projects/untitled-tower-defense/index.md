##### Project Description

This is more than a traditional tower defense game: its three levels offer three different ways to play.

{{< deeppink >}}**1 - Untitled Tower Defense**{{< /deeppink >}}

Level 1 is a traditional tower defense game with three waves, three turret types, and three enemy types.
Each turret can be upgraded once or sold.

{{< deeppink >}}**2 - Untitled Tower Attack**{{< /deeppink >}}

Level 2 lets players control the attackers in a third-person action game.

{{< /pfl-col >}}

{{< pfl-col >}}

{{< deeppink >}}**3 - Unlimited Random Tower Defense**{{< /deeppink >}}

Level 3 uses a procedural map generator driven by pathfinding algorithms.
After the third wave, enemies spawn randomly, and the game continues indefinitely with no final victory condition.

_Made with Unity._
